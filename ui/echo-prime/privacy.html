ECHO PRIMEWORKSPACE PRIVACY

Your data in Echo Workspace.

Updated November 1, 2026

Echo Prime Workspace stores conversations, projects, files, preferences and memories to provide a continuing assistant experience. This notice describes the workspace service and its sign-in flow.

Sign-in and account approval

Google sign-in provides an identity token containing account identifiers and a verified email address. The ECHO identity service verifies that token and matches it to an existing verified ECHO account. This sign-in client does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive or other Google content. Google identity information is used for authentication and account security.

New workspace accounts require the owner's approval. When Telegram approval notifications are configured, your account email and approval-request details are sent to the owner's configured Telegram chat for review. Telegram processes that notification. Account identifiers, email addresses, approval status and authentication audit records are stored to operate this process. Email/password sign-in is handled by the ECHO identity service; the workspace does not store your password.

Conversations and memories

Your account has separate storage for chats, projects, uploaded or generated files, preferences, memories and plugin connections. Other ordinary users cannot access that storage through their accounts. Service administrators operate the infrastructure and may have administrative access; this is not an end-to-end encrypted service.

Saved conversations and memories persist across sessions. The assistant may retrieve applicable information from your account and record preferences or memories to continue your work. Authentication and operational logs record service activity for security and debugging.

External services you use

Messages and relevant context are processed by the configured model service. Features such as speech, media generation, live web access and connected MCP plugins may send the text, audio, files, queries or tool inputs needed for that action to their configured providers. Connected providers may include ElevenLabs and the MCP server URLs you add. Their own privacy policies also apply. Review a plugin's provider and access before connecting it.

Plugin credentials are stored for your account to authenticate requests to the configured provider. Do not put passwords, API keys or other credentials into ordinary chat messages.

Sessions, network and retention

The canonical Workspace session cookie keeps you signed in. Prime also uses an HttpOnly same-origin interface cookie; that cookie does not grant access to a Workspace account. Appearance settings are stored in this browser. Private prompts and shared draft/history records are not stored in browser localStorage. The service processes network addresses and request metadata for routing, authentication and abuse prevention. Hosting and network providers may also process this connection information. Chats and memories remain saved until removed or handled through a deletion request; operational records and backups may retain copies. No automatic retention deadline is represented by this notice.

Contact and requests

For questions about your information or requests to access, correct or delete account data, contact echoomegaprime@gmail.com. Account verification may be needed before a request is processed.
